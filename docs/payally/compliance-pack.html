1. Balance — top-up and spend only, no withdrawals

All references to withdrawing, cashing out or transferring Balance have been removed from the website. Balance can only be topped up and used to pay for digital products on the platform; there is no withdrawal, payout or transfer function anywhere in the product or in the policies.

2. Privacy Policy — data retention

The Privacy Policy now sets a defined maximum retention period for every category of data, with automated monthly deletion, and states the backup retention explicitly. Periods follow UK and EU requirements.

Data categoryRetention
Account and profile data24 months after account closure or last interaction
Orders, invoices, Balance transactions, payment records 6 years from the end of the financial year (Companies Act 2006 s.388, HMRC record-keeping); up to 10 years where an EU member state's VAT rules require it. Delivered keys are masked after 12 months
Customer support and complaint correspondence24 months after the ticket closes, or until a related dispute ends
Fraud, chargeback and dispute evidence6 years (limitation period and card-scheme dispute windows)
Security, access and technical logs12 months; 30 days for routine logs without a security event
Password reset / email verification tokens1 hour / 24 hours, deleted on use or expiry
Marketing and consent recordsUntil opt-out or 24 months without interaction; hashed suppression record kept while marketing operates; cookie consent 12 months
Analytics and cookie data13 months
Backups and disaster recovery copies Encrypted, rolling 35-day cycle, automatically overwritten. Data deleted from live systems — including after an erasure request — disappears from backups within 35 days; during that window backups are restored only for disaster recovery

The policy also covers legal holds: where an erasure request or an expiring retention period conflicts with a statutory obligation, an unresolved dispute, a live fraud investigation or a limitation period, processing is restricted instead of deleted, the data is deleted once the obligation ends, and the customer is told that this applies.

3. Checkout

Both required elements appear on the payment screen, above the Pay button.

Card statement descriptor

Card statement descriptor: CartridgeClub is shown in the payment panel and repeated in the Seller & billing block (“The charge will appear on your card statement as CartridgeClub”). It is also stated in the Terms, Payment section, and on the Balance top-up screen.

Statutory withdrawal waiver

A mandatory checkbox, unticked by default, must be accepted before payment can be submitted. It is shown to every customer, not only to EU/UK customers, and it is enforced both in the form and on the server — a request without the acknowledgement is rejected. The same acknowledgement is required for a Balance top-up.

I understand that by purchasing digital goods and requesting immediate delivery, I waive my statutory right of withdrawal once delivery begins.

4. Refund Policy

The Refund, Cancellation and Withdrawal Policy has been reviewed against each item requested and contains:

  • Refund conditions and exclusions — invalid, already redeemed, revoked or misdescribed keys; non-delivery; pre-orders; third-party gift card codes; customer selection errors; and the delivered-key rule.
  • Technical failures — payment captured without a key issued, duplicate charge, or a delivery system error: redelivery or a full refund at the customer's choice; confirmed duplicate charges are refunded to the original payment method without deduction.
  • Response times — requests acknowledged within 2 business days, substantive decision within 14 days of receiving the information needed to investigate.
  • Submission window — refund and dispute requests within 30 days of purchase, longer where mandatory consumer law provides.
  • Chargeback procedure — contact support first, no duplicate recovery for the same transaction, handling of abusive chargebacks, without limiting the right to dispute an unauthorised transaction.
  • Billing contact — info@cartridge-club.com, linked in the technical-failure, cancellation, chargeback and dispute sections.
  • No contradictions on Balance — credits are non-withdrawable; reimbursement, where legally required, is made to the original payment method only.
